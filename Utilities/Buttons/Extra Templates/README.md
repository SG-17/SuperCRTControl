This folder contains various photoshop templates for use with Extrons.

## Extron Face Buttons
`Button Grid Official.psd` is the grid that Extron provides with their Button Label Generator program.  
`CRT.psd` is a template I made that has various combos of CRT icons with associated input types so you can mix and match to your desire.  
`Extron Crosspoint Ultra -- LABELS pg1.psd` and the other two pages are the button templates created by Dochartaigh for the original CRT-Control.  
`Extron Label Singular.psd` is a collection of buttons in layers on a 1x1 grid to make it easier to prep a button to load into a printable grid.  
`Monitor.psd` is a template I made that has various combos of monitor icons with associated resolutions so you can mix and match to your desire.  
`Tink.psd` is a template that I made that contains buttons for the various RetroTink devices.  
`Touchscreen Button.psd` is for use with Super CRT-Control to make new buttons that you may need.

> [!TIP]
> The buttons on Extrons that are compatible with Super CRT-Control are `0.5" x 0.5"`/`12.7mm x 12.7mm`/`300 x 300 pixels at 600 dpi` in size.
> You can print them out using any consumer printer and cut them out with an xacto (craft) knife.

## Installing Button Labels
1. To remove the button from the matrix switcher use a small, flat bladed screwdriver or a plastic pry tool to gently pry a button out from the front panel.
   - <img width="352" height="331" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d6c7dada-b463-41e6-825e-f66ecb017353" />
2. Using the same tool gently pry the diffuser (the white plastic backing) off of the clear button cap.
3. Remove the clear plastic button label from inside the plastic cap.
> [!WARNING]
> This label may fly or fall out on its own, however it is more likely to get stuck. Pressing the tip of your xacto knife into the inside corner of the label will most likely cause it to release and fall out.
4. Print and cut out the new labels you want to use.
5. Place them into the button cap and replace the diffuser.
6. Align the button with the empty socket on the Extron and firmly press it back into place, you should hear one or two clicks.
